      Data & storage

      What is the cache?

      Saved pages (the page cache) let a rescan within the reuse time read pages from disk instead of downloading them. That is what makes rescans fast. Clearing it makes the next scan of each company slower but fresh.

      Two small in-memory caches (DNS answers and parsed pages) are cleared with it. Your history, exports and backups are never touched.

      Back up automatically at launch
      Keeps the last 10 automatic backups. Only made when your data changed since the last one.

      Requests

      Pages are fetched with a browser-like client first; if it fails, plain requests are tried. Timeouts leave a request running in the background until it ends.
